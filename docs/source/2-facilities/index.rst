==========
Facilities
==========

.. _spaces-at-a-glance:

The KINESIS CTP Lab is organised into two primary operational areas:

- :doc:`Arena <arena>` — motion capture, robot testing, drone-safe experiments,
  and tracked motion studies
- :doc:`Workspace <workspace>` — development, prototyping, electronics work,
  storage, charging, and workstation access
