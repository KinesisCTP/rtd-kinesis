================
General Policies
================

Access & Authorization
----------------------

- Lab access is restricted to **authorized users only**. To request access, contact the lab manager — see :doc:`Processes </1-lab-overview/2-processes>` for the full procedure.
- Do not allow unauthorized individuals into the lab or hold doors open for them. Unauthorized entry is a safety risk for everyone in the space.
- Guests and visitors must be accompanied by an authorized lab member at all times.
- Access may be revoked for policy violations, safety incidents, or prolonged inactivity.

Safety Requirements
-------------------

- Complete all required safety training **before** operating any equipment. Training requirements vary by equipment — check each equipment page.
- Follow all posted safety instructions and warning signs.
- Wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) as required for the activity (closed-toed shoes and long trousers are minimum requirements in the Arena during operations).
- Report any incidents, near-misses, or equipment damage to the lab manager immediately, regardless of severity.
- Maintain a clean and organized workspace at all times. Clear your area after each session.
- Emergency exits must be kept clear and unobstructed at all times.
- Fire blankets are located near the main entrance and at the arena entry column — do not relocate them.

Equipment Use
-------------

- Only trained and authorized personnel may operate specialized equipment. Check each equipment page for specific training requirements.
- A **Risk Assessment** must be completed and approved before using equipment marked as requiring one. Contact the lab manager to initiate a risk assessment.
- Equipment must be used only for its intended purpose and within its rated specifications.
- Users are responsible for returning equipment to its designated storage location after use. Each equipment item has labelled storage in the equipment cabinets.
- Report any equipment malfunction, damage, or unusual behavior to the lab manager. Do not attempt to repair equipment yourself.
- Booking the equipment in advance is required for shared resources. Respect scheduled time slots.

Battery Safety
--------------

- All LiPo and Li-ion batteries must be charged only in the designated LiPo charging station.
- Never leave batteries charging unattended.
- Batteries not in use must be stored in the **fireproof battery storage cabinet**.
- Swollen, damaged, or deeply discharged batteries must not be used or charged. Dispose of them through NYUAD's hazardous materials process.
- Store batteries at approximately 50% state-of-charge for long-term storage.

Network & Data
--------------

- Do not connect unauthorized devices to the KINESIS CTP or Hermes networks without prior approval.
- Do not store sensitive personal data on shared lab workstations.
- Clean up large temporary files, datasets, and model checkpoints from shared workstations after your project concludes.
- Shared workstation resources (GPU, RAM, storage) should be used considerately. Schedule intensive jobs during off-peak hours when possible.
- All static IP address assignments must be documented in the IP Allocation page.

Compliance
----------

- All users must comply with NYU Abu Dhabi's institutional policies, including the Acceptable Use Policy for computing resources.
- Violations of these policies may result in suspension of lab access and referral to the appropriate university authority.
- These policies are reviewed periodically. Users will be notified of material changes.
