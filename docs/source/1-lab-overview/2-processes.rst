=========
Processes
=========

Requesting Lab Access
---------------------

1. Contact the lab manager (Samuel A. Prieto, sxp8070) to discuss your project needs and which equipment you intend to use.
2. Equipment-specific training will be scheduled. During the training session you will be walked through the equipment and provided with the relevant documentation — Risk Assessment, SOP, and manuals.
3. After the training, fill out the `CTP Training Attendance Sheet <https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScLW1MOvo-9aAwX2_04FcyLGPR9xtDso9hu9SEixUy2VzuAiw/viewform>`__ so the training is recorded in the CTP scheduling system.
4. If you need access to the physical lab space, complete all the training and reading outlined in the `Kinesis CTP Lab Safety Training and Risk Assessment Acknowledgment <https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSedhNihcdGHRwOLP31qAJ1eh_-v9jVnkLZiu3h1ZsYwxtNNlg/viewform>`__ form, then submit it. After submission, your access to the lab space will be processed by the lab team.

Booking Equipment
-----------------

Shared equipment (robots, scanners, drones) requires advance booking to avoid conflicts:

1. Check the `Core Labs Booking System <https://corelabs.abudhabi.nyu.edu>`__ from the NYUAD network or through the VPN.
2. Reserve your time slot with enough lead time -- typically at least 24 hours in advance for standard use, longer for complex setups.
3. Arrive on time. If you need to cancel, do so as early as possible so others can use the slot.
4. Extended or recurring bookings (e.g. for a research campaign) should be coordinated directly with the lab manager.

.. note::

   The booking system is only available from the NYUAD network or through the VPN.

Requesting a Static IP
----------------------

To connect a new device permanently to the KINESIS CTP network with a static IP:

1. Check the IP Allocation page for available addresses in the appropriate range.
2. Contact the lab manager with your device name, MAC address, and intended use.
3. Once approved, configure your device with the assigned IP and document it in the IP Allocation page.

Risk Assessments
----------------

Certain equipment and activities require a formal Risk Assessment before first use:

1. Identify whether your intended equipment or activity requires a risk assessment (check the equipment page).
2. Contact the lab manager to obtain the relevant risk assessment template.
3. Complete and submit the risk assessment for review and approval.
4. Keep a copy of the approved risk assessment accessible during operations.
5. Re-assess if the activity, environment, or equipment configuration changes significantly.

Data Management
---------------

- Store experimental data on the designated workstation or network storage -- not on robot onboard storage.
- Back up important datasets regularly. The lab does not guarantee backups of data stored on shared workstations.
- Clean up large temporary files after your project concludes.
- For long-term archival of research data, follow NYUAD's research data management policy.

Incident Reporting
------------------

Any incident (equipment damage, injury, near-miss, or safety concern) must be reported:

1. Ensure the immediate safety of all persons involved.
2. Notify the lab manager (sxp8070) as soon as possible.
3. Complete the applicable NYUAD incident report form.
4. Participate in any follow-up review or corrective action process.
